ROLL CALL/ SELF STUDENTS ATTENDANCE CHECKER

I came up with this design for students to be able to do their attendance on their own, Since sometimes it takes time for teachers or instructors mostly when the teacher is late or the student is late. I also came up with this for students not to disturb teachers while discussing, if there is a activity that is ongoing, and for teachers not to waste time on checking attendance on students

User Problem

When things go wrong, nothing is lost Four failure moments, each with a plain-language message, a safe state, and a manual way out.

A. Wrong tap Camille Santos marked Absent Undo What happens: every change shows Undo for 6 seconds. Tapping the selected status again clears it.

B. No connection You're offline. Marks are saved on this device and send when you reconnect. Reconnect What happens: work continues as normal. Changes queue locally and send on their own when the signal returns. Fallback: a Reconnect button lets the teacher retry by hand. No blocking spinner.

C. Sending fails Couldn't send attendance Your marks are safe on this device. Nothing was lost. Try again Export as CSV What happens: the message says what happened, what is safe, and what to do next. No error codes. Fallback: if the network stays down, export the roll as a CSV or print it.

D. If the student forgot to login It would trigger a warning or notification to the name of the student In 5 to 10 minutes it will notify the studenf for the student not to forget signing in or logging in his/her attendance

TARGET AUDDIENCE

Students

Highschool students to College Students

My target audience are students because this would be for students for them to be able to Self check on their attendance, I did not include elementary students because I think it would be hard for them or they would struggle especially the preschool students.

Chosen Aesthetic Theme

I chose light colors for students to be able to see and also teachers. With a balanced set of light colors it would be easier for the users to see and use it.

Interface goals

The goal of my interface is to keep track and for students to self check their attendance on subjects and on days. It's goal is also help the teachers or instructors because it takes time for them to check on their laptops and on papers.
